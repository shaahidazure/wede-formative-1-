About jxdn

Singer, songwriter, and pioneer of the modern pop-punk revival.

Who is jxdn?

Jaden Hossler, known professionally as jxdn, is an American singer and songwriter hailing from Chattanooga, Tennessee. After gaining massive online recognition, jxdn seamlessly transitioned into a full-time music career, quickly becoming one of the defining voices in the modern alternative rock and pop-punk revival movement.

In 2020, jxdn became the first artist signed to Travis Barker's DTA Records, releasing his debut single "Comatose", followed by breakout hits like "Angels & Demons" and "So What!".

Musical Style & Sound

jxdn’s music merges the high-energy electric guitar riffs and pounding drums of early-2000s pop-punk with modern hip-hop vocal cadence and introspective lyricism. His tracks often tackle topics such as mental health, heartbreak, personal growth, and resilience.

  • Genres: Pop-Punk, Alternative Rock, Emo Rap
  • Key Influences: Blink-182, Juice WRLD, Justin Bieber, Taking Back Sunday
  • Record Label: DTA Records / Elektra Music Group
